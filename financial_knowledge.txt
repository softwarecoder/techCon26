The Debt Snowball Method: Focus on paying off the smallest debt balance first to gain psychological momentum.
The Debt Avalanche Method: Focus on paying off the debt with the highest interest rate first to save money on interest.
Emergency Fund: A recommended emergency fund should cover 3 to 6 months of essential living expenses.
LATAM Context: In Latin America, inflation and fluctuating interest rates are common. Prioritize high-yield savings accounts if available.
Savings Rule: Aim to save at least 20% of your monthly income.
